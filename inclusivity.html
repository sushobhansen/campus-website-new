A Campus for All

Inclusive Features

Wheelchair-friendly Washrooms

Every academic and hostel building has a washroom that can be used by wheelchair-bound users

Ramps

All buildings and most footpaths have wheelchair-friendly ramps

Tactile Tiles

Most buildings have tactile tiles that are convenient for visually-impaired users

Wheelchair Slots

Our major auditoriums have special slots for wheelchair users

Thoughtful Rooms

Users with special needs are accomodated on the ground floor of hostels

Extensive Footpaths

Wide footpaths along most campus roads with adequate protection from traffic
