Housing

Not just a Workplace

A majority of faculty and staff stay on campus with their families. They form a vibrant community with engaging events organized by the Housing Management Committee (HMC). To get involved, email housingoffice@iitgn.ac.in.

Key Features

A variety of Apartments

435 apartments of sizes randing from 30 - 160 sq m

Kitchen Gardens

Ground floor apartments have a kitchen garden for use by residents

Community Area

Outdoor community area for social and cultural events, managed by HMC

Parking Spot

Every apartment has one dedicated parking spot, with additional parking available for visitors

Piped Natural Gas

Every apartment has a PNG connection - no LPG cylinders!

Maintenance Team

A dedicated maintenance team addresses civil and electrical concerns through the Maintenance Portal

Play Area

A well-equipped play area for children

Garbage Collection

Daily garbage collection between 08:00 AM and 10:00 AM, with e-Waste collected on Saturdays

School Buses

Buses of local schools, such as JNS and DPS, pick up and drop off children at designated stops
