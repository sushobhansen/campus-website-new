Animals on Campus

Animal Policies
  1. 1

    Pet dogs must be leashed at all times and owners are responsible for cleaning up after them

  2. 2

    Feeding or petting of dogs is strictly prohibited

  3. 3

    Stay away from wild animals

  4. 4

    In case of a bite, report to the Medical Center immediately for treatment

  5. 5

    To report any animal-related issue on campus, please write to amc@iitgn.ac.in
