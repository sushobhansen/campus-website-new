Campus Map

The map below is a definitive, searchable overview of major buildings and facilities on campus, developed and maintained by the Campus Development Office on top of Google Maps. Open the sidebar using the button on the top-left to scroll through the list. Alternatively, use the full-screen button on the top-right to open in Google Maps, which provides additional options for searching through the list and obtaining directions. In case you find any errors, please report them to office.cd@iitgn.ac.in
