Vehicle Rules for Residents

Rules

Drive with Care

Only campus residents and married students are allowed to keep vehicles on campus. Other students require approval from the Dean of Student Affairs.

Designated Parking

Every apartment has one designated parking in the housing area. Parking is also allowed on public parking lots. Do not park on the road.
Explore Campus Parking

Restricted Roads

Several roads have soft barricades, indicating that they should not ordinarily be used. Drive around the outer roads.

Follow the Rules

All vehicles must be registered with the RTO and all drivers must have a license. Follow all rules of the road applicable to India.

Speed Limit is 30 kmph

Drive at or below the speed limit. Speed traps are spread all over campus. Overspeeding can lead to disciplinary action.

Give Way

Pedestrians, bicyclists, and non-motorized vehicles have the right of way on all roads. Stop for them.

Keep Bicycles Safe

Note down the frame number of your bicycle and ensure that it is locked when unused.

Register your EV

A flat fee is charged for campus EV charging. If you bought an EV, register it at estateoffice@iitgn.ac.in

Do Not Blow Your Horn

Campus is generally a quiet zone. Do not blow your vehicle's horn unless it is necessary for safety.

Check your Email

Road closures are generally communicated by email beforehand by the Campus Development Office through the Housing Office.

Watch for Animals

The campus is shared with a number of animals. Drive safely and do not harm them.
Explore Campus Wildlife

Know your Gate

The campus has 3 entry gates, but you can't use all of them in the same way.
Explore Security Rules

Don't Block the Substations

Substations are required to have unimpeded access due to fire regulations. Don't block them.
Find Campus Substations

Use the Mirrors

Sharp turns and tricky intersections have concave mirrors to prevent road accidents. Use them while driving.
