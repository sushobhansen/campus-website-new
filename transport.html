Transportation

Reaching Campus

From Ahmedabad Airport

Take a taxi or an auto to IIT Gandhinagar, Palaj via Gandhinagar-Ahmedabad Road and Gandhinagar Bypass Road. About 23 km.

From Kalupur or Sabarmati Railway Station

Take a taxi or an auto to IIT Gandhinagar, Palaj via Gandhinagar-Ahmedabad Road and Gandhinagar Bypass Road. About 25-30 km.

From Gandhinagar Capital Railway Station

Take a taxi or an auto to IIT Gandhinagar, Palaj via KH Road and Gandhinagar Bypass Road. About 13 km.

By Metro Rail

From Dholakuva metro station or GIFT City metro station, take a taxi or auto to IIT Gandhinagar, Palaj via Gandhinagar Bypass Road. About 8 km.

By City Bus

Take GTS-7 bus from Pathikashram Terminal, Gandhinagar to IIT Main Gate
