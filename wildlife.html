Campus Wildlife

We Share Our Campus With

Indian Peafowl

Pavo cristatus, the national bird of India, is found in abundance on campus

Red-naped ibis

Pseudibis papillosa, often seen near the Central Arcade

Indian roller

Coracias benghalensis, a small bird often seen on trees and lightpoles

Laughing Dove

Spilopelia senegalensis, often watching you when you least expect it

Spotted Owlet

Athene brama, heard but not seen

Indian grey hornbill

Ocyceros birostris, louder than they look

(Information courtesy of Prof. Ambika Aiyadurai, Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, IIT Gandhinagar)

Animal Policies
  1. 1

    Pet dogs must be leashed at all times and owners are responsible for cleaning up after them

  2. 2

    Feeding or petting of dogs is strictly prohibited

  3. 3

    Stay away from wild animals

  4. 4

    In case of a bite, report to the Medical Center immediately for treatment

  5. 5

    To report any animal-related issue on campus, please write to amc@iitgn.ac.in
